\# RetailCo Business Analytics



End-to-end business analytics solution built on a real-world-scale retail e-commerce dataset spanning 4 years (2021–2024), 100,000 transactions, and 14,825 customers.



\## Project Overview



This project applies the full analytics lifecycle — from raw data quality assessment through descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics — and synthesizes findings into actionable business recommendations.



Built as part of BIT 5534: Applied Business Analytics and Intelligence at Virginia Tech.

\## Key Findings



\- 53.5% churn rate — more than double the 25% industry benchmark

\- 33% of customers generate 80% of total revenue (Pareto Analysis)

\- OLS Regression forecasting model achieves 2.14% MAPE — best of three models tested

\- Beauty \& Clothing cross-buyers confirmed 42% higher projected CLV than single-category buyers

\- December seasonal index of 1.36 confirms strong holiday demand pattern

\- Home \& Garden highest total annual inventory cost at $4,811 driven by unit cost not demand
